Nautilus Roofing, a Semple-Gooder Company, Launches Operations Across the Midwest

DETROIT, MICH. — Nautilus Roofing, a Semple-Gooder company, today announced its launch as a premier commercial and industrial roofing contractor serving clients throughout the Midwest.

Headquartered in the Detroit area, Nautilus Roofing combines local leadership with the expertise and resources of Semple-Gooder, one of Canada's leading roofing contractors. As Semple-Gooder continues to expand its North American presence, Nautilus brings specialized roofing knowledge and construction management expertise to commercial, industrial, and institutional clients across the region.

The company is led by Scott Nemecek, President of Nautilus Roofing, with Patrick Shain overseeing operations. Together with Mark Baxter, President of Semple-Gooder, the leadership team brings hundreds of years of combined roofing, construction, and project management experience to the business.

Nautilus Roofing was established to serve organizations requiring a trusted roofing partner capable of delivering complex projects safely, efficiently, and with minimal disruption to ongoing operations.

"The Midwest is home to some of North America's most important manufacturing, industrial, and commercial facilities," said Scott Nemecek, President of Nautilus Roofing. "Our team understands the unique challenges these facilities face and the importance of delivering roofing solutions that support long-term operational reliability."

Nemecek added, "By combining local expertise with the strength and experience of Semple-Gooder, we're able to provide clients with exceptional service, proven project execution, and the resources necessary to successfully manage projects of any size or complexity."

Nautilus Roofing offers a comprehensive portfolio of commercial and industrial roofing services, including new roof installations, re-roofing projects, preventative maintenance programs, emergency service response, and full-scale construction management. The company serves clients across a variety of sectors, including manufacturing, automotive, industrial, commercial, and institutional markets.

According to Mark Baxter, President of Semple-Gooder, the launch represents an important milestone in the company's long-term growth strategy.

"Nautilus Roofing reflects our commitment to bringing the experience, quality, and customer-focused approach that have defined Semple-Gooder for decades to clients throughout the Midwest," said Baxter. "With Scott Nemecek and Patrick Shain leading the organization, Nautilus is exceptionally well-positioned to support customers with reliable roofing solutions and professional project management from planning through completion."

Core Service Areas

  • New roofing installations for commercial, industrial, and institutional facilities
  • Re-roofing and roofing system upgrades designed to minimize operational disruption
  • Preventative maintenance and emergency service programs that protect roofing assets
  • Construction management services that ensure quality, safety, schedule adherence, and budget control

With strong local leadership, extensive industry experience, and the backing of an established North American roofing contractor, Nautilus Roofing is positioned to become a trusted partner for facility owners and property managers throughout the Midwest.

Meet Our President
Scott Nemecek
President, Nautilus Roofing

Scott Nemecek is President of Nautilus Roofing, a Semple-Gooder company, where he leads strategic growth, operational excellence, and client partnerships across the commercial and industrial roofing sector. With an extensive background in construction and business leadership, Scott has built a reputation for delivering high-quality projects, fostering strong customer relationships, and developing high-performing teams focused on safety, innovation, and long-term value.

Throughout his career, Scott has held leadership roles that have strengthened his expertise in project management, business development, operations, and customer service. His extensive experience across a broad range of construction markets—including institutional, automotive, federal, power and energy, petrochemical, municipal, and K-12 and higher education sectors—has provided him with a comprehensive understanding of complex building systems, large-scale project execution, and the evolving needs of commercial and industrial property owners.

Today, Scott leads Nautilus Roofing as it expands its presence throughout the Midwest, providing clients with comprehensive roofing solutions, construction management expertise, and exceptional service. Under his leadership, Nautilus continues to deliver dependable, high-performance roofing systems that help customers protect and maximize the value of their facilities.
